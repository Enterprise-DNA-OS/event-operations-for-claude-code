# Why Swoogo, and exactly what this replaces

Checked 10 October 2026. Swoogo is the comparison target for the new conference-operations family. We read the finished Supplier Assurance rebuild before authoring this one.

Swoogo lists Professional at $11,800 per year for one full user and one reporting user. Tax is extra where applicable. The vendor page HTML identifies USD in its currency indicator. This is a published starting subscription, not a verified customer invoice. [Pricing](https://swoogo.events/pricing/).

Scores in order: annual bill 2, data-shaped 4, our buyers 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 5, import path 5. Total 21/30. The public starting bill clears five figures. Buyer evidence is weak: no named customer purchase or invoice was verified in the available local records. Search results contain alternative comparisons, but search volume was not measured. [Alternative results](https://www.g2.com/products/swoogo/competitors/alternatives).

Bizzabo scored 19/30 (2,3,2,3,4,5). Its [pricing page](https://www.bizzabo.com/pricing) presents both a per-user annual commitment and a starting yearly price; we did not reconcile these into a made-up customer bill. Cvent scored 17/30 (1,3,2,4,3,4). Its [pricing page](https://www.cvent.com/en/event-management-software/cvent-pricing) requests a quote, so its annual bill remains unverified. These are judgement scores, not market measurements.

Swoogo's [registration report update](https://swoogo.events/product-updates/track-whats-changed-in-your-invitation-registration-report/) confirms CSV export. Its [registrant reference](https://swoogo.readme.io/reference/getallregistrants) documents names, contact details, registration status and event identifiers. Export headings vary; the importer accepts common headings, documented field names and explicit mappings. The fixture is synthetic and is not a captured vendor export.

This base handles internal event records, allocations, speaker deadlines, sponsor fulfilment, attendance and paperwork. It works without an attendee website because an operator imports or records registrations. Payments, participant self-service, badge hardware, mobile apps, campaigns and integrations are separately scoped delivery work. We neither take payments nor implement regulated payment processing.

Swoogo already offers custom reporting and [conversational access](https://swoogo.events/mcp/). The ten questions in this project are demonstrated capabilities, not claims that Swoogo cannot answer them. We have no evidence for an exclusive capability claim.
